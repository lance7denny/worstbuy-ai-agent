# Refund Policy

## General
Refund cannot exceed amount paid. Ambiguous/conflicting/insufficient evidence goes to human review.

## Damaged item
Full refund/replacement may be allowed within 30 days. Photo evidence is required before automated refund.

## Missing parcel
Check carrier status. Confirmed loss/no successful delivery may be refunded. Delivered-but-disputed goes to human review.

## Wrong item
May be refunded within 30 days after verification. Photo evidence of the item received is required for verification. High-value/ambiguous cases go to human review.

## Automation controls
Hard automation cap is $200. Auto-approval threshold is $75. Higher-value, repeated-refund, conflicting, failed-tool, or ambiguous cases go to human review.

## Replacement
A damaged, wrong or lost item may be replaced within 30 days of delivery when stock is available. Damaged items need photo evidence before an automated replacement. If the item is out of stock, offer a refund instead. Replacements above $75, wrong-item claims above $75, repeated refund history (3 or more in 12 months), delivered-but-disputed parcels and ambiguous cases go to human review.

## Partial refund
A claim reported within 14 days of delivery is eligible for a full refund. A claim reported 15 to 30 days after delivery is eligible for a 50% partial refund.

## Not covered
Requests made more than 30 days after delivery are not eligible. Change of mind, wrong size or "does not fit" are not covered by this policy and are declined. A claim that contradicts the order record (for example damage reported on an order still in transit) goes to human review.
